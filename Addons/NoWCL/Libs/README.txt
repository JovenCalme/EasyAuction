Bundled third-party libraries
=============================

NoWCL embeds the following libraries so its appearance settings do not depend
on Details or another addon being installed:

- LibStub
- CallbackHandler-1.0
- AceComm-3.0 revision 1333 (minor 14)
- ChatThrottleLib 29 (Public Domain)
- LibSharedMedia-3.0 (LGPL v2.1)

The appearance libraries are copied unmodified from the versions bundled with
Details. AceComm-3.0 and ChatThrottleLib retain their upstream source,
copyright, author, revision, and license notices; only line endings and
trailing whitespace are normalized for this repository. The LibSharedMedia
license is available at:
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
